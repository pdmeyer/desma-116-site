linkTitle: "Assignment 6a"
---

Using the skills you have already learned about Ableton and the sounds you have
designed through the field recording and physical sound design projects, make a
short sound composition about 1 minute in length.

Requirements:

- You must use at least 4 of the sounds from the sound library you have been
  building in this course
- Use the timeline to bring the elements in and out of the composition
- Use the mixing and panning controls to create a sense of dynamics and space

Upload your completed composition to the course server.
