This is prep for [Assignment 4b - Field Recording](../assigment4b).

1. Read the assignment for 4b
2. Find sonic environments that you think will be a good fit for the
   requirements of 4b.
3. Note these environments. If you like, you can take a quick phone recording
4. Make a document that roughly sketches out your plan for Assignment 4b. Note
   the places you will visit, what time you will go, what you expect to hear,
   and what requirements of Assignment 4b you think each environment will satisfy.

Come to class on the date this assignment is due prepared to talk about your
plan.
