Using a handheld recorder from the department (or, if necessary, your phone),
create 8 field recordings of various types.

- At least one recording should be a relaxing, calm, placid environment.
- At least one recording should be an exhilirating, overwhelming, or oppressive
  environment
- At least one recording should be taken outdoors
- At least one recording should be taken indoors
- At some point across all four of these recordings, you must capture:
  - A continuous sound (e.g. a drone or hum)
  - A repetitive or rhythmic sound
  - An intermittent or transitory sound (e.g. a sound that appears only once or
    that recurs but not with any discernible pattern or rhythm)
  - A metallic sound
  - A wooden or hollow sound
  - A creaky or crinkly sound
  - A bassy sound

Each recording should be its own WAV file, uploaded to the course server. You
don’t have to worry about editing these sounds for now - we’ll do that together
next.

**Separately, make a document:** For each sound, describe where you took the
recording, when you took it, what equipment you used, and which of the
qualities above apply to that sound.
