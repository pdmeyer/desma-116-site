Assigned Class: Class 5 Due Class: Class 9

In this assignment, you will take a piece of audio and create your own visual
accompaniment. You can make your visual elements using any technique that you
like. Like with our writing assignments, we’re trying to capture the ideas,
associations, and feelings of a sound, then translate that into another medium.
Use the prompt questions from [Assignment 1 - Focused
Listening](../assignment1) to guide your thought process as you consider what
to make.

The length of the piece depends. If your piece is complex and tightly
synchronized to the sound, then something as short as 5 seconds would be
suitable. Something that’s longer, more generative and ethereal might be as
long as 2 minutes.

You should also be prepared to explain your approach during critique, drawing
when possible upon the topics we have encountered in reading and in class.

Here is some audio that you can use for your pieces:
<https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1T6U9JatpK6d-AY3NMHeI61mX2dOkPA6O>.
Some of these are quite long, so feel free to grab a fragment. If you need help
editing one down to size, let us know.

If you would like to use audio that your source yourself, let us know.
