Our final project builds upon the skills we’ve developed over the course of the
quarter. In Class 15, you will submit a proposal that describes your plan for
the final project.

You have two options for the final project:

1. Make a (set of) sound composition(s). This is a sound-only composition,
   preferably made with many of the sounds you have designed in this course.
   8-12 minutes in length total across 1 or more pieces. You can use as many as
   you like.
2. Sonify or soundtrack an existing work. Make a sonic accompaniment to an
   existing work that you have made in the past or are currently making for
   another class.
3. Something else, subject to approval. I’m definitely open to other approaches
   here - just let me know in advance if you think you are going this
   direction.

Your proposal should answer these questions:

1. What is the subject of the piece? What concepts, themes, or ideas does will
   this piece investigate?
2. How do those themes translate into artistic or aesthetic decisions? What
   qualities will your sounds and your compositional structure use?
3. What specific techniques, sounds, or approaches will you use to execute your
   piece? (Of course, this may change once you start working, and that’s fine,
   but do your best to guess).

If you choose to make the sonic accompaniment to another artwork, your proposal
should elaborate on the relationship between the sonic and other senses in your
work. How do the different media interact to achieve your artistic objectives?
