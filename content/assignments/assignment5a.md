This is prep for Class 11 - Foley and [Assignment 5b - Physical Sound
Design](../assignment5b)

1. Read the assignment for 6b
2. Find objects that you think sound cool! They can be objects around your
   house, things you find on the street, materials you have access to
3. Bring the objects to Class 9

Some inspiration:

- Resonant objects like ceramic, plastic, or metal vessels
- Sheets of plastic or metal that make a "wobbling sound"
- Rubber, silicone, and other stretchy materials
- Cloth that you can rub or tear
- Machines like a printer
- Materials of different textures (sandpaper, for example
